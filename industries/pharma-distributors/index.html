Why it matters

Your buyer checks you out before they reply.

A purchasing manager at a clinic group, a hospital procurement officer, a pharmacy chain buyer — they get approached constantly, and the first thing they do with an unfamiliar supplier is look you up. Not to admire the design, but to answer one question: is this a real, credible company I can put on an approved-vendor list? A thin or dated site answers that badly, and the email you spent weeks earning gets silently ignored. In distribution the website isn't the lead source — it's the credibility check that decides whether your outreach converts at all.

The leak most distributors don't see

A 3,000-name list is not a pipeline.

Most B2B outreach fails on arithmetic, not effort. A big unfiltered list gets blasted, response rates crater, the domain reputation takes damage, and the whole channel gets written off as "email doesn't work for us." It does — but only after the list is ranked. The work that moves the number is upstream: figuring out which few hundred accounts are actually worth contacting, then testing the message against them until it lands. That's the difference between sending more and selling more.
