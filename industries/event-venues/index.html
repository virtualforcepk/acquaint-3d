Why it matters for venues & exhibitors

The pipeline is built before the doors open.

A trade show is where deals get delivered, not where they get found. By the time the floor opens, the exhibitors with full booths already booked their meetings — they worked a list of the right stakeholders for weeks beforehand. Venues run the same clock in reverse: the planner who books your room in March started shortlisting in January. Waiting on the inbound enquiry means competing for whoever's left after everyone else has already reached out. The teams that stay booked don't wait — they run outbound, on a schedule, all year.

The system behind a full booth

Outreach, email, and follow-up — run as one machine.

What moves the number isn't sending more, it's sending to the right people and never dropping the thread. We build the list of stakeholders who genuinely matter — event planners, procurement contacts, exhibitor marketing managers — rank it so your team's hours go to the accounts worth having, then run a tested email sequence against it with automated follow-up and every reply tracked in one place. The same discipline that keeps a 10+ person event team on schedule, pointed at your pipeline instead of your run sheet.
